About The Club

The Legacy of Mighty Tonj North

Tonj North County Football Team, affectionately known as Mighty Tonj North, is more than just a Football Team,it is a symbol of community pride, strength, and resilience from Warrap State, South Sudan.

Featuring our iconic black and yellow colors and inspired by our motto "In Unity we Trust", our club brings together elite local talents from Tonj North County to compete at the highest levels of regional and national football tournaments hence empowering our youth and uniting our community through the beautiful game.
